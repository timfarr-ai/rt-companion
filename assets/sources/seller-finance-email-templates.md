# Filters to Find Seller Finance Deals 

  
Consider adding a “vacant” filter. Often times a seller will need the proceeds from the sale of a house they live in to buy their next property. For that reason it’s best to look at vacant properties that have been on the market for 150+ days. You can go after condos, multi family, and single family houses using this strategy.
