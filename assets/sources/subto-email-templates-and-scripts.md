# 2nd Objection The “Can I qualify for a new loan?”

  
After selling your property subject to the existing mortgage, the mortgage stays in the seller’s name. Normally when you sell your property the mortgage is paid off. Because of this real estate agents and sellers may ask you if they will be able to qualify for a new loan.   
The real answer is that it depends on their debt to income ratio currently. Any car loans they’re paying, credit card debt they have, etc. will be taken into consideration when qualifying for a new loan. This combined with interest rates being higher than they’ve been in 20-25 years means that most Americans can’t qualify for a loan regardless.

# In Depth Trust Email

  
Good Afternoon Adam,   
Thank you for taking the time to speak with us today about our offer. In essence, we'd like to take over your client's debt in the safest and cleanest way possible.   
The structure of the deal is quite simple, we'd pay for a trust to be created, put the property into a trust and have a title company process the sale of the trust.   
The trust provides a number of protections and conveniences to your client. First and foremost, it prevents the loan from being called "due on sale." According to the Garn St. Germain Act of 1982.   
Second, it allows them to reset their debt to income ratio and qualify for a third loan. Your client will hold 10% interest in the trust. When you own part of a trust that's receiving income, the debt can be written off of your DTI. In the eyes of the loan underwriter, we are responsible for the debt as we hold the majority interest of the trust.   
Thirdly, this structure allows them to reclaim the property without having to foreclose. It's entirely possible that I'm hit by a bus and can no longer make the payments. This could have a negative effect on their credit score. As a protection, the language of the trust specifies that if 2 mortgage payments are missed, the property defaults back to your client. We cannot circumvent this language within the trust and reclaiming the property is as simple as making a call to the attorney that closed this.
