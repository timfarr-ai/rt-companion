# Cash Arbitrage / Section 8 — Canonical SOP

> Single source of truth for the cash buy-&-hold play. Provenance: `assets/CAPTURED_SOURCES.md` #6 +
> `assets/sources/cash-section8-course-transcript.txt` (new Section 8 / Cash course).
> One of 5 strategies. *(The cash course: "two types of cash deals — Section 8 and Fix & Flip"; Fix &
> Flip is its own SOP. This covers the buy-&-hold / Section 8 / cash-arbitrage side.)*

**What it is:** a property that **already cash-flows for a buyer** (often a **Section 8 turnkey rental**) —
don't force creative; make a **cash offer at a discount** and wholesale it to a buy-&-hold investor
(DSCR-financed). Two flavours: **distress Section 8** (needs light work) and **turnkey Section 8** (rent-ready).

---

## 1. CRITERIA
- **Determination:** cash-flows at/near retail for an investor (creative CF > ~$300/mo, or a clean
  Section 8 rental) → cash play, not seller finance.
- **Section 8 sweet spots:** FL · TN · IN (high Section 8 FMR). Property rent must be **≤ FMR** to qualify
  (usually a given at the target price/location). Best **< $150K** (DSCR cash-flows on cheap at 6%+ rates).
- **Discount:** rent-ready **sub-20% off**; distress more. (vs Fix & Flip's 30%+ / ARV math.)

## 2. COMPS
- **Load-bearing = value + rent.** Confirm **FMR** (HUD, by zip + bedroom count) for the Section 8 rent,
  and value via comps so the cash discount is real. `CF = rent×0.80 − PITI` must clear for the DSCR buyer.

## 3. MATH / OFFER
- **Cash offer at a discount to value;** the spread is your assignment fee. Buyer's economics: **DSCR loan**
  (20% down, investment property; works best < $150K, need cheap to cash flow at 6%+).
- **Section 8 upside:** government pays **~30% ABOVE market rent** + guaranteed → strong, stable CF for the
  buyer (why "some of the wealthiest landlords are Section 8 investors").
- Be pessimistic; the discount must add value over what the buyer could offer themselves.

## 4. SCRIPT
- Cash + speed (same finality as Fix & Flip), but the thesis is **rental cash flow**, not rehab-and-flip.
- **DSCR offer email** (Drive Cash folder): Purchase Price · 10-business-day inspection · EMD $1,500
  non-refundable post-inspection · **COE 45 days** (DSCR clears in 4-6 weeks) · *"open to creative terms
  if your client is open to them"* (pivot hook).
- Qualify condition + Section 8 status (current voucher tenant? HQS-passing?). Title company of their choice.

## 5. EMAIL TEMPLATES
- **DSCR offer email** + **sample cash-offer email** (Drive `5 - Cash Arbitrage/` "Cash Deals Discord
  Content"). Cross-links to the SF + MT email decks for the creative-pivot fallback.

## 6. CONTRACT
- **Use the realtor's STATE-APPROVED contract** for traditional Section 8 / cash deals (ask the agent to
  draft). Your own docs (Drive `5 - Cash Arbitrage/`): CASH PURCHASE AGREEMENT, Purchase Contract, LOI,
  Addendum, Termination (TREC), Assignment / JV / Partial Payout. **Double close** if assignment barred.

## 7. TERMINOLOGY
Section 8 (HUD voucher; ~30% above-market guaranteed rent; 20-yr waitlists) · **FMR** (Fair Market Rent,
HUD, by zip+beds) · **HQS** (Housing Quality Standards, annual inspection) · **DSCR loan** (debt-service-
coverage; 20% down; investment props; <$150K) · double close · assignment fee · proof of funds · holding
costs · title company · turnkey vs distress.
