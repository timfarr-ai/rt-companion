# Sub-To — Playbook

Seller has a mortgage and **almost no equity**. You take over their existing loan payment, hand them a
small lump (often **$0**), and the loan stays in their name inside a trust. Fast, no new financing.

**Use it when:** there's a loan + the seller nets **≤ $15K** at a sale (cornered). Call **lowest-equity
first** — that's the most motivated seller.

## STEP 2 — Verify before you dial

Confirm three things on the property: **interest rate**, **loan balance**, **loan type**.
Skip it if the loan is a **HELOC or reverse mortgage** — those don't work.

🔧 PropStream (rate + liens + loan type) · [Propwire](https://propwire.com/search) (free, partial)

## STEP 3 — The call

**Open — ask about the loan (say this):**

> "If you've got a mortgage on it, I'd be happy to just take over your payments — a servicing company
> pays it for you so you never have to worry about it. If a payment is ever missed, the property comes
> right back to you and you keep everything paid up to that point. Since I'd run it as a rental, I just
> need the rate and balance to make sure it cash-flows — can you confirm those so my offer is solid?"

**The Trojan Horse (if the agent won't entertain a takeover):** make a cash offer designed to be
**rejected** — `loan balance + 4% of asking` (nets the seller ~$0). When they say the seller can't make
money at that price, you act surprised and pivot:

> "There may actually be a creative way for your seller to walk away clean without losing money — would
> you be open to hearing it?"

→ then give the takeover/trust pitch.

**Objections — say exactly this:**

> **"Subject-to is illegal."** → "It's standard — line 203 of the HUD-1 settlement statement is literally
> 'existing loan(s) taken subject to.' It's in Modern Real Estate Practice, Unit 12."
>
> **"The bank will call the loan (due-on-sale)."** → "We put it in a land trust, so title moves to the
> trust, not a new owner — the bank has no trigger. They don't call performing loans."
>
> **"What if you stop paying?"** → "If I miss two payments the property reverts to your seller and they
> keep every payment I've made. I've got real money in it — I'm not walking."
>
> **"Can my seller buy again after this?"** → "Yes — I give them a trust certificate and an occupancy
> agreement showing they're covered. It resets their debt-to-income so they can qualify again."

## STEP 4 — The offer

| Existing rate | Cash to seller |
|---|---|
| 6%+ | **$0 down** |
| under $150K price | **$5K–12K lump** |
| pricier | **~5% down** |

Seller monthly ≈ **$0** (you pay the bank, not the seller). Run the deal + get the go/no-go:

🔧 **[Offer Oven Calculator (Sub-To)](/rt-companion/playbooks/offer-oven-calculator.html?otype=Subto)** —
the card auto-fills it; it returns **PROCEED / COUNTER / KILL**.

**Who buys it (set this now):** if it cash-flows at the existing payment → an **investor**. If it doesn't
(pricey + high rate) → **$0 down and sell to someone who wants to live in it.** Both are real deals.

---

## STEP 5 — Get it signed

**Trust Acquisition** (this is the contract): the house goes into a revocable living trust via a trust
attorney; the **mortgage stays in the seller's name**; you get **90–95%** of the trust, the seller keeps
**5–10%**; you make the payments. Plus an **Assignment Disclosure** so you can assign it.

## The email — send right after the call (copy-paste, fill the $$)

```
Subject: [Address] — taking over the payments

Good evening [Name], thank you for your time today.

My lender won't budge much on [$130,000] — your seller would still lose $5–10k on a straight sale.
So instead I'll just take over their debt and let them qualify for a new loan down the road:

  • I take over whatever's owed on the mortgage and keep it current
  • [$8,500] to you and [$7,000] cash to your seller
  • I cover all closing costs

The loan isn't assumable, but here's the legal loophole: your client puts the property into a trust
created by an attorney, and sells me the trust. It's a legal sale and it resets their DTI completely.

Can we present this so your seller walks with [$7,000]? My full portfolio and proof of funds are in
your text messages.

Regards,
[You]
```
