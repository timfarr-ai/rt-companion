Cash Arbitrage / Section 8 — Playbook

A property that already cash-flows for a buyer — usually a Section 8 rental. Don't force creative: make a cash offer at a discount and assign it to a buy-&-hold investor (who finances with a DSCR loan). The spread between your discounted price and what the investor will pay is your assignment fee. Two flavours: turnkey Section 8 (rent-ready) and distress Section 8 (needs light work).

Qualify: cash-flows at/near retail for an investor, in a strong Section 8 market, cheap (best < $150K — a DSCR buyer needs cheap to cash-flow at 6%+ rates). Turnkey should rarely be over ~$85K — past that the buyer's cash flow gets thin.

 1 FIND  →  2 VERIFY FMR + RENT + CF  →  3 CALL  →  4 OFFER  →  5 EMAIL  →  6 SIGN  →  7 SUBMIT

Step 1 — Find

Open the Call Sheet → Cash section, or search BBC Lightning Leads. Section 8 sweet spots: FL · TN · IN (also OK / Memphis / Indianapolis) — these markets pay high Section 8 rents. Look for cheap properties that are already rented or rentable to a voucher tenant. "These new Section 8 landlords have no idea where to find deals — that's great news for us."

Step 2 — Verify the Section 8 rent + real rent + cash flow

The whole edge is the government rent being above market — so confirm it directly:

  1. FMR — look up the HUD Fair Market Rent for the zip + bedroom count. ⚠ Don't trust the listing's rent claim — "the listing might say $14,500; you call the Section 8 department and they say $1,200." Verify with the actual Section 8 housing authority. More bedrooms = higher voucher rent.
  2. Value — confirm via comps so the cash discount you're asking for is real.
  3. Cash flow — CF = rent × 0.80 − PITI must clear for the DSCR buyer (20% down, 6–7% rate).

Step 3 — Call

Same cash-offer opener as Fix & Flip — never say "wholesaler" — but the thesis is rental cash flow, not rehab:

"I work with investors who buy rentals — cash, close fast. I don't have an agent on this one, would you be open to dual agency? I'm mainly interested because of the strong Section 8 rents in this area."

Cash + speed is your finality:

"Cash means no financing contingency and no chance of falling out of contract. I can close as fast as your title company moves."

Qualify the Section 8 status: is there a current voucher tenant? Is it HQS-passing (it gets an annual inspection)? Leave a hook for a creative pivot if the numbers force it:

"If a clean cash number doesn't work for your client, I'm also open to creative terms — let me know."

Step 4 — Offer

Cash offer = value − discount      (the spread between this and the DSCR buyer's price = your fee)
  • Discount: rent-ready turnkey sub-20% off; distress more. (Lighter than Fix & Flip's 30%+ ARV math — the value here is the cash flow, not the rehab upside.)
  • Buyer's economics: DSCR loan — 20% down, investment property, works best < $150K.
  • Section 8 upside: the government pays ~30% above market rent, guaranteed — strong, stable cash flow (it's why some of the wealthiest landlords are Section 8 investors). That's the story you're selling the end buyer.
  • Be pessimistic — the discount has to add real value over what the buyer could offer themselves.

Run the discount math + get the verdict (use the Fix & Flip / ARV mode for the spread): Offer Oven — Cash / Section 8 → returns PROCEED / COUNTER / KILL.

Step 5 — Email the offer

Call first, then send the cash offer. Note the longer COE — DSCR financing clears in 4–6 weeks — and keep the creative pivot in (fill the [brackets]):

Good Afternoon [Agent],
Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today about your listing on [address]. I'd like to present
a cash offer via your representation — my proof of funds is attached.

- Offer Price: [$95,000]
- EMD: $1,500 non-refundable, submitted post-inspection
- Inspection Period: 10 business days
- COE: on or before 45 days (this gives my DSCR financing room to clear)
- Title Company: title company of your choice

I'm a buy-&-hold investor, so I'm also open to creative terms if your client would prefer payments over a
lump sum — just let me know what works best for them.
Regards, [You]

Step 6 — Get it under contract

Use the realtor's STATE-APPROVED purchase agreement (primary). This is a traditional cash deal — ask the agent to draft their state's standard purchase contract. Don't bring your own purchase contract.

Your own Drive docs cover the assignment/exit side: Assignment + JV + Partial Payout agreements, LOI, Addendum, Termination Agreement (TREC), and the CASH PURCHASE AGREEMENT (only if no agent contract is available). Double close if assignment is barred.

Funding (end buyer's DSCR / your POF): Roy / Prime Investor Solutions — hard money + DSCR purchase & refi loans, proof-of-funds letters.
