# Fix & Flip — Canonical SOP

> Single source of truth for Fix & Flip (cash). Replaces `fix-and-flip-cash.md`. Provenance:
> `assets/CAPTURED_SOURCES.md` #6 + `assets/sources/fix-and-flip-course-transcript.txt` (new course).
> One of 5 strategies. The **volume engine** (~⅔–¾ of all wholesale deals are cash).

**What it is:** cash purchase of a **distressed, discounted** property; assign to a flipper/investor.
You win on the **buy**, not on terms. Fastest cycle (verbal yes → close 14-21 days).

---

## 1. CRITERIA
- **Determination:** BBC `fixAndFlip` tag, or distress demotion (pre-1940 + <$80K, or vision/flip-relist
  signals). Cheap, **needs work**, priced below comps. Keywords: *"as-is," "investor special," "fixer."*
- Code gate: DOM 60+, **< $250K** (Richard's live examples cluster $99K-$200K Detroit/MI/OH).
- **The signal:** a house listed ~$190K surrounded by ~$300K comps = opportunity. Outdated interiors.

## 2. COMPS  *(load-bearing = ARV from SOLD comps)*
- **ARV ritual (canonical):** SOLD comps ≤ 6 months, within **0.3 miles**, match beds/baths/sqft, built
  within **15-20 years**, **fully-renovated comps only**. Average **3** by $/sqft, **avoid outliers**
  (e.g. $315 / $263 / $250 → use $276/sqft). Multiply by subject sqft. Confirm condition on Realtor.com.
- **Rehab estimate:** roof $8-13K · kitchen $7.5-10K · garage $9-22K · electrical $3.5K+ · **default $50K**
  if unsure · **round UP** · add 10-20% contingency. Get a contractor walk before committing.

## 3. MATH / OFFER
```
MAO (Max Allowable Offer) = (ARV × 0.70) − Rehab − Assignment Fee     (some also subtract holding/closing)
e.g.  ARV $300,000 → ×0.70 = $210,000 − $50,000 rehab − $10,000 fee = $150,000
```
- **70% is the CEILING, not the target** — offer **60-65%** of ARV in practice (Richard offered $72K where
  the rule allowed $95K). **Be pessimistic on both ARV and rehab.**
- **Discount rule:** at least **$30-50K below list** (more if pricey). *"No discount = no deal"* — $10K
  below list adds no value (the buyer could've offered that themselves).
- ⚠ **Triage note:** the card's "70% of LIST" is a **placeholder** — the real MAO is ARV-based and needs
  the comp ritual (ARV isn't in BBC data). Card should drive the operator into the ARV/rehab step.

## 4. SCRIPT
- **Cash = finality, not negotiation.** *"I don't need to inspect, so there's no chance of going under
  contract and canceling. Cash, close in 14 days."* (verbatim). Short voicemail (~20-30s).
- **Realtor approach — never say "wholesaler":** *"I work with investors buying 2-4 fix & flip homes per
  month. We close fast and pay cash. Would you be open to dual agency if I don't have an agent?"* (lets
  them earn both commissions → accepts your lower offer).
- **Get the low offer accepted:** send **POF** + your **ARV comps list** + a realistic **rehab estimate** +
  ask for **7-day inspection** & **EMD after inspection**.
- **Never negotiate up from your number.** No → next deal in 2 minutes. Proof of funds same-day.

## 5. EMAIL TEMPLATES
- Sample **cash-offer email** (Drive Cash folder): comp-comparison framing (*"renovated 3/1 $80K vs
  unlivable 3/1 $85K — what would you rather have?"*) · Price · EMD $1,500 non-refundable post-inspection ·
  7-day inspection · COE 30 days · title company of their choice. **Call first.**

## 6. CONTRACT
- **Use the realtor's STATE-APPROVED purchase contract** — ask the agent to draft it (they can represent
  you). Don't bring your own for traditional deals.
- Your own docs (Drive `5 - Cash Arbitrage/`): **CASH PURCHASE AGREEMENT**, REAL ESTATE PURCHASE & SALE
  template, **LOI template**, **Addendum** (more inspection time / add terms), **Termination Agreement**
  (TREC), **Assignment** + **JV** + **Partial Payout** agreements. **Double close** if assignment is barred.
- **Funding (buyer/self):** Roy / Prime Investor Solutions — hard money + DSCR (Office-forms apps).

## 7. TERMINOLOGY
ARV · MAO (`ARV×0.7 − repairs − profit`) · hard money loan (value-based, 6-18mo, fast, covers purchase +
rehab) · assignment contract / fee · double close · scope of work (SOW) · holding costs · rehab budget ·
contingency clause · exit strategy · proof of funds (POF) · comps · title company.
