# Fix & Flip — Playbook

A **cash** purchase of a **distressed, discounted** house, assigned to a flipper. You win on the **buy**,
not on terms — get it cheap enough that the flipper still makes money. This is the **volume engine**:
roughly two-thirds to three-quarters of all wholesale deals are cash. Fastest cycle — verbal yes to close
in 14–21 days.

**Qualify:** cheap, **needs work**, priced below comps, sitting on market (DOM 60+, usually < $250K). The
signal is a house listed ~$190K surrounded by ~$300K renovated comps. Keywords: *"as-is," "investor
special," "fixer," "needs TLC."*

```
 1 FIND  →  2 COMP ARV + REHAB  →  3 CALL  →  4 OFFER  →  5 EMAIL  →  6 SIGN  →  7 SUBMIT
```

---

### Step 1 — Find

Open the **[Call Sheet](/rt-companion/briefings/latest.html)** → *Fix & Flip* section (the `fixAndFlip`
tag, or distress signals: pre-1940 + cheap, flip-relist). Or search
[BBC Lightning Leads](https://www.buyboxcartel.com/vip/lightning-leads). Richard's live examples cluster
**$99K–$200K** in Detroit / MI / OH — outdated interiors, below-comp list price.

### Step 2 — Comp the ARV + estimate rehab

This is the whole game — ARV isn't in BBC data, so you build it yourself.

**ARV ritual:** pull **SOLD** comps from the last **6 months**, within **0.3 miles**, matching beds / baths
/ sqft, built within **15–20 years**, and **fully renovated only** (confirm condition on Realtor.com).
Average **3** of them by **$/sqft**, throwing out outliers (e.g. $315 / $263 / $250 → use ~$276/sqft), then
multiply by the subject's square footage.

**Rehab estimate:** roof $8–13K · kitchen $7.5–10K · garage $9–22K · electrical $3.5K+ · **default to $50K**
if you're unsure · **round UP** · add a **10–20% contingency**. Send your contractor first (lockbox code's
on the MLS) — *"in flipping, you send your contractor to the property before you make the offer."* **Be
pessimistic on both ARV and rehab.**

### Step 3 — Call

**Never say "wholesaler."** Open as a cash investor and offer the agent dual agency so they earn both
commissions (and will push your lower offer through):

> "I work with investors who buy 2–4 fix-and-flip homes a month. We close fast and pay cash. I don't have
> an agent on this one — would you be open to dual agency so you'd represent both sides?"

Cash is your finality, not a negotiation:

> "Cash means there's no financing to fall through and no chance of going under contract and canceling on
> you. I can close in 14 days. I'd just want my contractor to walk it first so I can firm up my number."

**Get the low offer accepted** by stacking proof: send **proof of funds** + your **ARV comps list** + the
**realistic rehab estimate** + ask for a **7-day inspection** with **EMD after** inspection. Keep
voicemails short (~20–30s).

**Never negotiate up from your number.** If it's a no, the next deal is two minutes away.

### Step 4 — Offer

```
MAO (Max Allowable Offer) = (ARV × 0.70) − Rehab − Assignment Fee
e.g.  ARV $300,000 → ×0.70 = $210,000 − $50,000 rehab − $10,000 fee = $150,000
```

- **70% is the CEILING, not the target.** In practice offer **60–65%** of ARV (Richard offered $72K where
  the rule allowed $95K).
- **Discount rule:** at least **$30–50K below list** (more if pricey). *"No discount = no deal"* — $10K
  below list adds nothing, the buyer could've offered that themselves.

Run the discount math + get the verdict:
**[Offer Oven — Fix & Flip](/rt-companion/playbooks/offer-oven-calculator.html?mode=ff)** (enter ARV +
rehab + fee) → returns **PROCEED / COUNTER / KILL**.

### Step 5 — Email the offer

**Call first**, then send the short cash-offer email (fill the [brackets]):

```
Good Evening [Michael],
Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today about your listing on [Roanoke Ave]. I'd like to
present an offer to purchase via your representation. Could you send over the seller's disclosure when you
get a chance? My proof of funds is attached. Here's where I'm at:

- Offer Price: [$150,000]
- EMD: $2,000 non-refundable, submitted post-inspection
- Inspection Period: 7 business days
- COE: on or before 30 days
- Title Company: title company of your choice — happy to use mine if you don't have one

Regards, [You]
```

Want to frame the discount? Add the renovated-vs-unlivable comparison: *"a renovated 3/1 nearby sold for
$80K; this one needs full work and is listed at $85K — what would your client rather have, a real cash
buyer at a fair number or another 90 days on market?"* Full library:
[Cash / Fix & Flip email deck ↗](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1faQLOfVvmOvZ5Yl4K9k6_wUvGCfbTwyMs2J6x7Mr7Kw/edit)

### Step 6 — Get it under contract

**Use the realtor's STATE-APPROVED purchase agreement (primary).** This is a traditional cash deal — ask
the agent to draft their state's standard purchase contract (they can represent you). **Don't bring your
own** purchase contract to a traditional deal.

Your own Drive docs are for the assignment/exit side, not the purchase itself: **Assignment** + **JV** +
**Partial Payout** agreements, **LOI**, **Addendum** (to add inspection time / terms), **Termination
Agreement** (TREC), and the **CASH PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (only if no agent contract is available). **Double
close** if the contract bars assignment.

**Funding** (for yourself or the end buyer): **Roy / Prime Investor Solutions** — hard money + DSCR,
proof-of-funds letters, Office-forms applications.
