# Strategy Asset — Document Template (use one per strategy)

Create **5 folders** in your Richard Taylor Drive (under a `Strategy Assets/` parent), then build a Google
Doc in each following the structure below. Same six sections for every strategy — duplication across
strategies is fine and expected.

```
Strategy Assets/
  ├── 1 - Seller Finance
  ├── 2 - Sub-To
  ├── 3 - Hybrid
  ├── 4 - Fix & Flip
  └── 5 - Cash Arbitrage
```

**Golden rule:** every section starts with a **Source stamp** — where the content came from, who, when.
That stamp is what makes it defensible. Example:
`Source: HMHW "Seller Finance" course module 3 · Richard · pulled 2026-06-11`
`Source: Discord #deal-check pin by <leader> · 2026-03-xx`
`Source: Steve Ward 1:1 call · 2026-06-05`
`Source: house-built (not HMHW-canonical) — drafted from Steve's skeleton`

Drop any real **contract / LOI files** (PDF/DOCX) straight into the folder next to the doc.

## DOCUMENT STRUCTURE (copy this into each strategy's Google Doc)

### 1. Criteria — *who qualifies, and the test that routes a lead here*
> Source: ____
- The determination rule (what makes a lead THIS strategy vs another).
- Property profile: type, price band, units, DOM, equity/loan situation.
- The disqualifiers (what kicks it out / re-routes it).
- Any thresholds with the number + where Richard states it.

### 2. Comps — *what you must verify before offering*
> Source: ____
- The load-bearing comp for this strategy (RENT? ARV? both?).
- The exact ritual (e.g. haircut asking rent to real average; sold-comps only).
- The pass/fail line (e.g. CF = rent×0.80 − PITI > 0; ARV spread ≥ $X).

### 3. Script — *the call*
> Source: ____
- Opener (voicemail + live).
- The core pitch / one-liner (verbatim if Richard has one).
- Qualifying questions in order.
- Top objections + rebuttals.
- Definition of a "verbal yes" for this strategy.

### 4. Email — *post-call follow-up*
> Source: ____
- First-contact email (if used).
- Follow-up / recap email (reference the actual conversation).
- LOI cover note.
- *If you don't have a canonical one, paste the closest HMHW example and mark it; else we house-build it.*

### 5. Contract — *the paperwork* ⭐ highest priority (zero on file today)
> Source: ____
- The actual contract / LOI / agreement for this structure (drop the file in the folder).
- Note which clauses are strategy-specific (e.g. land-trust / due-on-sale for Sub-To & Hybrid;
  carry-back note for SF & Hybrid; no-contingency / proof-of-funds for cash).

### 6. Terminology — *the language*
> Source: ____
- Key terms a seller/agent will hear and what they mean (e.g. due-on-sale, land trust, DSCR,
  balloon, assignment, wrap, carry-back, subject-to).
- Any "say it like this / don't say it like this" notes (e.g. never say "subject-to" out loud).

## What each strategy's doc is really about (so the content stays on-target)

| Strategy | Criteria spine | Comp that decides | Contract you need |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seller Finance** | free & clear; Checkmate (MFH/non-retail) or Stale-SFH | **RENT** | SF carry-back / promissory note + purchase agreement |
| **Sub-To** | existing loan, ≤$15K equity-after-costs (cornered) | ARV (size equity) + existing PITI | Subject-To / **trust-acquisition** agreement |
| **Hybrid** | existing loan + real equity + rate ≤6% | ARV **and** rent | assume + **carry-back note** combo |
| **Fix & Flip** | distressed, <$250K, win on the buy | **ARV** (sold comps) | cash purchase agreement (no contingency) |
| **Cash Arbitrage** | already cash-flows; wholesale at a discount | ARV / value | cash purchase + **assignment** agreement |
